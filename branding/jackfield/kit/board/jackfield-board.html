The brief

A local control plane for the skills (and soon MCP servers) that AI coding agents load. For developers running 2-4 agents who need to see what each one has, spot what drifted and fix it safely. It should feel like a calm, exact instrument.

precisetrustworthycalmengineered

Readable at 16 px, works in one colour, light and dark first-class, distinct from AI dev-tool cliches (sparkles, gradients, node networks, hexagons).

Final design

Quiet soft J

A 3x3 field of jacks: the cells that spell J are lit, and the blue one is the jack being patched in.

  • Mirrors the product's own skills x agents table, with the active cells lit
  • Reads as a J at every size; below 24 px the grey cells fade and it becomes the small cut automatically
  • Every cell edge sits on a whole pixel at 16 px; one radius (8) plus one soft radius (40)

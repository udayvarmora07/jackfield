Jackfield

A 3×3 field of jacks. The cells that spell J are lit, the quiet cells stay grey, and the blue cell is the jack being patched in. It's the product's own skills × agents table with the active cells lit.

01 · Construction

A field on a 16-pixel grid

  • Canvas 256. Cells 64, gaps 16, margin 16. Every cell edge lands on a whole pixel at 16 px.
  • Two radii. 8 on every cell; 40 on the bowl's two outer corners, so the J reads as a letter.
  • Dark files are thinner. Light cells are inset by 1 unit to offset irradiation, the effect that makes light shapes on dark grounds look heavier.

02 · Clear space

One cell on every side

Keep a quarter of the symbol's height clear around the logo, measured from the outer cells. The lockups use the same unit between symbol and wordmark.

03 · Versions

Lockups, light and dark

  • Horizontal is primary: app header, README, website.
  • Stacked for square spaces: social cards, stickers, splash screens.
  • Symbol for icons and avatars.
  • Wordmark: IBM Plex Mono SemiBold, outlined. The i and j dots are field cells, and the j's is the blue jack.

04 · One colour

The J carries it alone

One-colour files drop the quiet cells and keep the J with its jack. Use them for print, embroidery, laser etching and any ground where the colours fail, such as a blue background.

05 · Minimum size

A small cut below 24 px

  • Symbol: 24 px with quiet cells; the small cut (no quiet cells) from 24 down to 16 px.
  • Horizontal lockup: 120 px wide. Stacked: 80 px wide. Wordmark: 72 px wide.
  • The favicon is the small cut and follows the browser's light or dark setting.

07 · Don'ts

Keep the field intact

  • One blue cell, always the top-right jack.
  • The J stays upright: no rotating, mirroring or rearranging cells.
  • Dark grounds get the dark or white files, never the light ones.
  • No gradients, shadows, glows, outlines, stretching or retyped wordmarks.
